03 About

A technical writer who also builds.

I'm Pryme — a technology-focused creator working at the intersection of technical writing, AI-assisted development, design, and Web3.

Writing is where I start. Most technical products fail not because the technology is weak, but because no one explained it clearly. That's the gap I work in.

AI-assisted development lets me go beyond describing a product to actually prototyping it. Design sharpens how it's communicated visually. Web3 gives me a technical niche where clear explanation is especially rare.

I use AI as a force multiplier, not a replacement for judgment — the thinking stays mine.
